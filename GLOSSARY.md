# Kanso

Kanso finds and tests multi-year breakouts in NSE equities. This glossary is the project's own vocabulary. For general trading terms, see the primer in `docs/v0/glossary.md`.

## Language

**Evidence**:
The typed set of numbers behind one condition's verdict on one bar, such as the level, the level date and the volume ratio. Each condition type has its own shape of evidence.
_Avoid_: details, metadata, info
