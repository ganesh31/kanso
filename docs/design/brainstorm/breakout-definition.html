What counts as a "multi-year breakout" to you?

Pick every shape you'd call one (more than one is fine). Each becomes a pattern in the library, so this decides what the scanner looks for first.

A

New N-year high

Close goes above the highest high of the last 3/5/10 years. One simple threshold, no shape required.

B

Tested ceiling

A level that rejected price 2+ times over the years finally gives way. Number of touches matters.

C

Long base / consolidation

A big earlier run, then years of sideways range (rectangle, cup), then price leaves the box. The base length is the point.

D

P&F multi-year resistance

On a Point & Figure chart, an X column clears a top that has held for years (double/triple-top buy at a multi-year level).

What makes you trust one breakout more than another?

Pick all that apply. These become the "quality" filters you can stack in the system builder.

1

Volume surge

Breakout day/week volume well above average

2

Close confirmation

Needs a weekly or monthly close above the level, not just an intraday poke

3

Tightening before the break

Swings get smaller near the ceiling (VCP-style)

4

Relative strength

Stock beating Nifty / its sector over recent months

5

Sector breaking out too

Several stocks in the same sector clearing old highs together

6

Not extended yet

Still within a few % of the level, so the stop is close

7

Fundamentals

Earnings / sales growth behind the move
