What to borrow, what to do differently

Borrow from RZone

  • Numbered conditions + an expression (1 and (2 or 3)). It allows OR and brackets, which my all-AND sketch can't. I'd keep plain-English blocks but add this as an "advanced" box.
  • Indicators and patterns in one picker. RZone even has a "High above previous N bars" pattern. That's our N-year high (3 years ≈ 750 trading days).
  • Saved, named systems you can reuse in the scanner.

Do differently

  • Exits live inside the system. In RZone a system is a filter; when to sell is set elsewhere. A breakout system without exits can't be judged.
  • One screen, not three menus. Same rule → backtest and scan with one click each.
  • Show the honesty assumptions (fill price, costs, survivors) next to the results.
  • Stock report explains why: the evidence behind each ✅/❌.
